>Basically this disorder is all about extra worrying  in which a person experiences excessive and uncontrollable worry about many everyday situations, even when there is little or in fact no real reason to worry (kinda overthinker)  &#x9;
Although its not that of a life threatening but still it can definitely interfere with the daily life so we can't really ignore it right can we? So before everything first we need to check if a person is suffering through it or not right?
Now there are several games, using their scoring we can identify to some extent.
Games(rounds should be random)
\---->**Dot-Probe Task**: basically we will use reaction time for scoring, two words will be used, one on right, one on left which will stay for abt 0.5 sec then the words will gone and two dots will appear one on left one on right and question will asked abt  one of the word and the participants have to click the dot/arrow either right or left side acc to the given word as fast as possible and the reaction time will be recorded, let say flower(right), knife(left) then the question comes , flower! Or  knife!, if choosing the negative words like knife takes less time and at the end if the average time of choosing the negative word is less than the positive words than………..:)
**Questions?**
flower     	knife
q)tell which side was the flower!
a)dot on left(reaction time noted:3sec pos=pos+3)
or
q)tell which side was the knife!
a)dot on right(reaction time noted:1sec neg=neg+1)
